I am an Associate Research Fellow at the Korea Institute of S&T Evaluation and Planning (KISTEP) and a Ph.D. candidate in Public Administration (Public Policy) at Seoul National University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of public policy and science, technology, and innovation (STI) policy, with a particular focus on public R&D investment, research collaboration, and regional innovation. I am interested in how government policies and public investments shape the behavior of firms and research institutions, and how these processes relate to innovation outcomes.

My research has examined participatory budgeting, the alignment between science and technology policy priorities and R&D investment, and the geography of research collaboration. I am also interested in how firms' financial characteristics influence their participation in government-funded R&D programs and subsequent innovation outcomes.

At KISTEP, I conduct policy-oriented research on national R&D investment, research performance, and regional innovation strategies, using administrative data and quantitative methods to address policy-relevant questions.

## Research Interests

- Public Policy and Governance
- Science, Technology, and Innovation Policy
- Public R&D Investment and Innovation Outcomes
- Research Collaboration and Regional Innovation
