## Participatory Budgeting and Local Government Integrity

This study examines the relationship between citizen participation in participatory budgeting, digital engagement, and local government integrity in South Korea.

Using panel data from 236 local governments covering 2016–2022, the study investigates whether participation in local budgeting and the use of digital platforms are associated with differences in government integrity. The findings suggest a positive association between citizen proposal activity and internal government integrity, while digital participation did not show a statistically significant positive relationship with integrity.

*Presented at the Korean Association for Policy Studies (2023) and the Korean Society for Technology Innovation (2024).*

## Pandemic Proximity: The Geography of Research Collaboration During COVID-19

This project examines whether the COVID-19 pandemic altered the geographic patterns of research collaboration in South Korea's national R&D system.

Drawing on national R&D project data, the research investigates changes in the spatial structure of collaboration and their implications for understanding how research partnerships respond to major external disruptions.

*Poster presentation scheduled for the 2026 APPAM Annual Research Conference.* [Poster page](/bio/appam2026/)

## Regional R&D Investment, Specialization, and Performance in Korea

This project examines the distribution, composition, and performance of public R&D investment across South Korea's 17 regions.

Using national R&D investment and performance data, the analysis explores regional research specialization, investment portfolios, and differences in research outcomes, with implications for evidence-based regional innovation strategies.

## Emerging Research Interests

I am interested in the relationship between government R&D support, firm financial conditions, and innovation outcomes, particularly how liquidity and financial constraints may influence firms' participation in publicly funded R&D programs and their subsequent innovation activities.
